Helping students make sense of renewable electricity.

EduGrid is an educational website concept designed to make electricity demand and renewable generation easier to explore. Instead of only reading charts and large figures, students can test simple scenarios and discuss what changes.

Explore the interactive demo ↗See how it works

The prototype uses fictional teaching values. It is not connected to live electricity data and does not predict Ireland’s electricity supply.

SECTION 01

The problem and the user

Electricity statistics can be difficult to interpret when they are presented only as abstract numbers, charts or technical reports.

The problem

Students may learn that wind and solar are important to the energy transition, but it can be hard to visualise how renewable output and electricity demand relate to each other. Static figures can make the topic feel distant from everyday life.

The intended users

EduGrid is aimed at secondary-school teachers and students in Ireland. It could support classroom discussion, data interpretation and introductory learning about renewable energy and the electricity system.

SECTION 03

How it works

A possible classroom journey through the EduGrid concept.

STEP 01

Start with a question

For example: what might happen to a simplified renewable coverage score if wind output drops?

STEP 02

Change one value

Move one slider while keeping the other values fixed, so the comparison is easier to interpret.

STEP 03

Compare and explain

Record the result, compare scenarios and discuss why this model is not a real grid forecast.

SECTION 04

Existing resources and alternatives

EduGrid would complement—not replace—trusted energy information and teaching resources.

Grid information

EirGrid

Publishes information about Ireland’s electricity transmission system and related system data. EduGrid’s proposed role is to provide a simpler classroom activity, while learners can consult official sources for real information.

Energy education

SEAI

Provides energy and sustainability information and educational resources. EduGrid could sit alongside those resources as an interactive scenario-based learning aid.

SECTION 05

Proposed business model

This is an early concept rather than an operating service.

Keep the core classroom experience accessible

A potential future version could offer free basic activities for students and teachers, with optional teacher resources, lesson packs or school-level features if there were evidence of demand. Any real business model would need to be tested with users and designed around school budgets and accessibility.
